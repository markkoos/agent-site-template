## Start with the four things every buyer notices

1. **Clutter** — pack a third of what's on shelves and counters.
2. **Cleanliness** — professional deep clean, including windows.
3. **Light** — open blinds, replace burnt bulbs, use matching warm-white bulbs.
4. **Smell** — neutral is the goal; skip heavy air fresheners.

## Room by room

### Kitchen
- Clear counters except one or two items.
- Fix dripping faucets and sticky drawers.
- Consider updated hardware or light fixtures.

### Bathrooms
- Re-caulk tubs and showers.
- New towels, bath mat and shower curtain in light colors.

### Bedrooms
- Remove extra furniture so rooms look larger.
- Neutral bedding; clear nightstands.

### Outside
- Mow, edge, trim and add fresh mulch.
- Paint or replace the front door hardware and house numbers.

## What to skip

Big remodels, new flooring in good condition, and highly personal upgrades. Ask your agent which items buyers in your price range actually pay for.
