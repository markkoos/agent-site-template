## What "off-market" really means

An off-market (or quiet, or private) listing is a home that is for sale but not publicly advertised on the MLS or consumer portals. Instead, the listing agent shares it directly with selected buyers and agents.

## When it makes sense

- **Privacy.** High-profile sellers, or anyone who doesn't want photos of their home online.
- **Testing price.** Gauging response before a public launch.
- **A known buyer.** When a likely buyer is already in the picture.

## The trade-offs

Fewer buyers see the home, so there is less competition. Days on market and price history may also look different once a home eventually goes public.

## Check the rules first

Many MLSs have policies about how quickly a listing must be shared once it is marketed to the public. Ask your agent how your MLS's current rules apply before deciding.
